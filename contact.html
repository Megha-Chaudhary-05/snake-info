🚑 Medical Emergency & Helpline

In case of a snakebite or snake-related emergency, remain calm and seek immediate medical assistance. Do not attempt to suck out the venom or apply a tourniquet. Call the nearest emergency service or visit the closest hospital as quickly as possible.

🩹 Snakebite First Aid

  • ✔ Stay calm and keep the victim still.
  • ✔ Remove rings, watches, or tight clothing near the bite.
  • ✔ Keep the bitten limb below heart level.
  • ✔ Reach the nearest hospital immediately.
  • ❌ Do NOT cut the wound or suck out the venom.
  • ❌ Do NOT apply ice or a tight tourniquet.
  • ❌ Do NOT try to catch or kill the snake.
