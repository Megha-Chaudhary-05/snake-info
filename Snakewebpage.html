Snake Scale Gallery

Smooth scales give snakes a glossy appearance and help reduce friction during movement.

Keeled scales contain a raised ridge that gives snakes a rough texture and improves camouflage.

Ventral scales are found on the belly and help snakes grip the ground while moving.

Head scales protect sensitive areas and are often used to identify different snake species.
